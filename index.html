Joko · Buildability

Joko represents the ability to turn ideas into working products people can use. A good idea needs a practical first build.

“A good idea is an idea that can be built.”

Kowi · Originality

Kowi represents the courage to make something with its own identity. A different approach matters when there is a reason behind it.

“Being different is not just about standing out, but having a reason to be different.”

Dodo · Clarity

Dodo represents simplicity in explaining an idea. The problem and solution should make sense to both the people building it and the people using it.

“A great idea becomes more meaningful when everyone can understand it.”

Joko builds, Kowi differentiates, and Dodo clarifies. Together, they look for an idea people can build, recognize as its own, and understand.

    Publishing

    How publishing works

    The pitch you write here never leaves your browser on its own. Publishing writes a record to the public BOT Chain ledger: the idea name, the pitch, and the three scores, stamped with your wallet. You only need a connected wallet; your wallet pays its own small network fee.

    What goes on-chain

    • Your wallet address as the proposer
    • The idea name and the pitch text
    • The three reviewer scores and the total
    • The timestamp

    What stays off-chain

    • The reviewers' written reasons behind each score
    • The analysis dimensions behind each score
    • Your conversation with the reviewers
    • Anything you did not confirm for publishing

    How to publish

      On-chain records are public and permanent. Do not publish private, legal, medical, financial, or other high-stakes information. AI feedback is an impression, not advice.
